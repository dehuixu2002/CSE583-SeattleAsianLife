## User Story 1

Who is the user:Emily Chen is a first-year undergraduate student at UW who recently moved to Seattle.

What do they want to do with the tool:She wants to find a Chinese restaurant where she can have dinner with three friends after class.

What need and desires do they want for the tool:She wants a clean restaurant within a 15-minute walk from campus, with seating for four people, meals under $20 per person, and a menu with pictures.

What is their skill level:She uses Google Maps regularly but has never used a restaurant food safety inspection tool.

## User Story 9

Who is the user：A new UW student who is unfamiliar with Seattle.

What do they want to do with the tool： Find Asian restaurants, cafés, grocery stores, and cultural or recreational activities near campus.

What needs and desires do they have for the tool： They want listings organized by category, with useful descriptions, addresses, and map links. They prefer a clear interface that helps them discover places without already knowing the neighborhood.

What is their skill level： Beginner with local knowledge; comfortable using websites and mobile apps.


## User Story 10

Who is the user： A student who wants to manage a limited budget.

What do they want to do with the tool： Compare places by estimated price range or typical per-person cost before deciding where to go.

What needs and desires do they have for the tool： They want price information to be easy to compare and clearly identified as an estimate or user-reported amount, with the date when it was last updated if available.

What is their skill level： Beginner to intermediate; comfortable using filters and sorting.


## User Story 11

Who is the user： A student who considers food safety when choosing a restaurant.

What do they want to do with the tool： Check a restaurant’s available public food inspection information.

What needs and desires do they have for the tool： They want to see the inspection status, date, and source, with a plain-language explanation. They want official inspection information to be distinguishable from user reviews.

What is their skill level： Beginner; needs information presented in clear, nontechnical language.

## User Story 12

Who is the user： A student who relies on public transportation, walking, or biking.

What do they want to do with the tool： Find places that are convenient to reach from campus or their current location.

What needs and desires do they have for the tool： They want nearby transit stops, approximate walking distance, and a map or directions link. They also want any neighborhood safety context to include its source and date.

What is their skill level： Beginner to intermediate; familiar with maps and transit apps.


## User Story 13

Who is the user： A student who sometimes drives to destinations.

What do they want to do with the tool： Find parking locations near a restaurant, grocery store, café, or activity.

What needs and desires do they have for the tool： They want parking locations and rates, plus clearly labeled occupancy or availability information when it exists. They do not want historical estimates to be presented as live parking availability.

What is their skill level： Beginner to intermediate; comfortable using map-based tools.


## User Story 14

Who is the user： A student planning an outing with friends or a student club.

What do they want to do with the tool： Combine several stops, such as an Asian grocery store, a restaurant, and a cultural activity.

What needs and desires do they have for the tool： They want to compare locations, travel convenience, and estimated cost, then view the stops together on a map. They prefer a simple way to share the plan with friends.

What is their skill level： Beginner to intermediate; comfortable browsing and sharing links.


## User Story 15

Who is the user：A student who wants to share a recent experience and learn from other students.

What do they want to do with the tool：Write a review and read reviews about places they are considering.

What needs and desires do they have for the tool： They want reviews to include useful details such as visit date, price paid, and category-specific ratings. They want to sort reviews by recency and understand that reviews reflect individual experiences.

What is their skill level：Beginner; able to use a basic form and rating controls.


## User Story 16

Who is the user: A project team member or data curator responsible for keeping the guide accurate.

What do they want to do with the tool: Add or update place listings and review imported public data.

What need and desires do they want for the tool: They want to identify duplicate or outdated listings, record data sources and update dates, and separate official records from community-submitted content.


What is their skill level: Intermediate to advanced; comfortable working with spreadsheets or structured data.
